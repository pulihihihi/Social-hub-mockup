## Summary
A compact objective pack for Delta Force publishing tasks in SocialHub.

## Key Facts
- New Post AI should optimize for publish-ready operator output, not for generic long-form brainstorming.
- Common post objectives include awareness, community reaction, participation, watch time, and campaign attachment.
- The AI layer should reduce operator effort on metadata, formatting, and standards compliance first.

## How To Use
- Use this to prioritize concise, action-ready copy and to keep generated content aligned to post execution rather than abstract brand theory.
