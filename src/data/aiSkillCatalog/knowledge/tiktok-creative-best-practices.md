## Summary
Official creative guidance that helps adapt Delta Force copy for TikTok-first packaging.

## Key Facts
- TikTok recommends vertical 9:16 creative, clear CTA, and platform-native production choices.
- Short-form captions should support the first seconds of the video rather than restate every visual detail.
- AI should keep TikTok copy punchier than Facebook or YouTube copy.

## How To Use
- Use this to tighten hooks, CTA shape, and vertical-video framing language.
