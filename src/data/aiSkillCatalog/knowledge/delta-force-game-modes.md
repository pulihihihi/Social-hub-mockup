## Summary
This package breaks Delta Force into the game modes and fantasy pillars most relevant to social publishing.

## Key Facts
- Official store copy highlights extraction-style missions with Operators, boss encounters, loot, and exfil decisions.
- Steam also highlights Warfare as 64-player combat with land, sea, and air vehicles.
- Mode-aware copy should separate tactical squad extraction, gear progression, and all-out warfare spectacle.

## How To Use
- Use mode language to decide whether a post should focus on survival tension, loot progression, vehicle combat, or operator ability fantasy.
