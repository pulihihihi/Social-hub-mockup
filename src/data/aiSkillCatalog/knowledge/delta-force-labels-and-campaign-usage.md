## Summary
Knowledge for selecting only labels and campaign values that already exist in the local SocialHub stores.

## Key Facts
- The label picker is driven by managed tag collections and must stay inside that vocabulary.
- Campaign selection must use active campaigns from the local campaign store.
- AI should prefer Delta Force VN, Video, Campaign, Community, and Event-like labels when they fit the content.

## How To Use
- Use this to make tag and campaign suggestions that are immediately applicable inside the UI.
