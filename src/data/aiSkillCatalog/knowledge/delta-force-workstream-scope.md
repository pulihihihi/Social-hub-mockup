## Summary
A workstream reference so generated recommendations can stay inside reasonable owner boundaries.

## Key Facts
- Publishing content can depend on product, marketing, creative, community, or regional workflow input.
- AI should recommend the lightest workflow that still fits the visible post risk and content type.
- Escalation-heavy recommendations should be limited to cases where the content implies risk or compliance review.

## How To Use
- Use this knowledge to choose workflow owners, campaign framing, and review pressure.
