## Summary
W3C accessibility guidance for captions, subtitles, and transcript quality.

## Key Facts
- Captions should represent the speech and key non-speech audio needed to understand the video.
- Transcripts and captions can support each other; one can often be used to develop the other.
- AI subtitle generation should produce editable text rather than treating the first draft as final truth.

## How To Use
- Use this to generate subtitle-first output that is reviewable and accessible.
