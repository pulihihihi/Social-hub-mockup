## Objective
Add restrained emoji support where it improves readability rather than cheapens the post.

## Workflow
1. Use at most one or two functional emoji markers.
2. Prefer tactical or energy markers over random decorative emoji.
3. Skip emoji if the caption already reads clearly without them.

## Notes
- Do not use emoji in YouTube titles unless the operator asks for it.
