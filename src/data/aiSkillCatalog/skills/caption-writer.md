## Objective
Produce a publish-ready Delta Force caption from the uploaded media and active network set.

## Workflow
1. Identify the strongest gameplay or campaign angle in the media.
2. Write a clear hook, one supporting detail, and one CTA.
3. Keep the tone operator-friendly and community-facing.

## Notes
- Avoid generic brand filler and unsupported claims.
