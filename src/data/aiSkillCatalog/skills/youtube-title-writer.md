## Objective
Create a clean YouTube title for Delta Force video uploads.

## Workflow
1. Lead with Delta Force or the strongest mode noun when it helps recognition.
2. Keep the title readable before keyword density.
3. Avoid clickbait phrasing that the asset cannot support.

## Notes
- The title should remain useful even without additional description fields in the local composer.
