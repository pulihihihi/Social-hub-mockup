## Objective
Adapt a base caption into a more TikTok-native short-form version.

## Workflow
1. Lead with the strongest operator or clip hook.
2. Keep the copy shorter and more kinetic than feed captions.
3. End on a clear prompt or reaction CTA.

## Notes
- Use this when TikTok is in the selected network set.
